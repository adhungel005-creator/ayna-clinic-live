About Ayna Clinic

Ayna – The Skin, Hair & Laser Clinic represents a new era of dermatological care in Nepal. We bridge the gap between traditional medicine and modern aesthetic technology. Our clinic is designed to be a haven for skin health, utilizing FDA-approved lasers and sterile diagnostic environments.

We specialize in treating chronic conditions such as Melasma, Eczema, and clinical Hair Loss, ensuring every patient receives a comprehensive analysis before treatment begins. At Ayna, your skin health is our scientific mission.
