About Ayna Clinic

Ayna – The Skin, Hair & Laser Clinic is founded on the principle of providing evidence-based dermatological care. We believe that every patient deserves a customized treatment plan that combines the latest medical breakthroughs with ethical practice.

Located in Biratnagar, our facility is equipped with state-of-the-art laser technology and follows strict hygiene protocols to provide a safe, comfortable, and luxury clinical experience.

  • FDA-Approved Procedures
  • Sterile Clinical Environment
