Frequently Asked Questions

Does laser hair removal hurt?

Our FDA-approved laser technology features advanced cooling mechanisms, making the procedure virtually painless.

How long is the downtime for a Chemical Peel?

Mild peels have little downtime, while deeper peels may require 3-5 days of peeling and recovery.

Is the consultation fee adjustable?

The consultation fee is a standard charge for Dr. Baaniya's assessment. It may be adjusted if proceeding with a treatment package.

Is laser hair removal permanent?

It results in long-term hair reduction. Occasional maintenance sessions may be required.

Does chemical peel make skin thin?

No, it actually stimulates collagen production, making the dermis thicker over time.

Stuffs to avoid in melasma

Avoid direct sun exposure, extreme heat, and harsh exfoliants.

Food to avoid in acne

Avoid high glycemic index foods, excessive dairy, and processed fast foods.
