Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting

Does laser hair removal hurt?

Our FDA-approved laser technology features advanced cooling mechanisms, making the procedure virtually painless and highly comfortable compared to traditional methods.

How long is the downtime for a Chemical Peel?

Downtime depends on the depth of the peel. Mild peels have little to no downtime, while deeper peels (like Nomelan Fenol) may require 3-5 days of peeling and recovery.

Is the consultation fee adjustable?

The consultation fee is a standard charge for Dr. Baaniya's expert clinical assessment. However, it is often adjusted or waived if you proceed with a treatment package on the same day.

Is laser hair removal permanent?

Laser hair removal significantly reduces hair growth, but it is typically considered long-term hair reduction rather than 100% permanent. Occasional maintenance sessions may be required.

Does chemical peel make skin thin?

No, when done correctly by professionals, chemical peels actually stimulate collagen production, making the dermis thicker and stronger over time, even though it temporarily removes the dead outer layer.

Stuffs to avoid in melasma

Avoid direct sun exposure without high SPF sunscreen, extreme heat, harsh exfoliants, and scented skincare products that can irritate the skin and trigger pigmentation.

Food to avoid in acne

It is generally recommended to avoid high glycemic index foods (like sugary sweets and white bread), excessive dairy (especially skim milk), and heavily processed fast foods to help manage acne flare-ups.
